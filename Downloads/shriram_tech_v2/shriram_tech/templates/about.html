Our Story & Mission

Shriram Technologies is a registered partnership firm established with a mission to provide comprehensive technology, automation and engineering solutions to businesses across India and abroad. We bring together deep domain expertise in industrial automation, robotics, software, AI and manpower solutions under a single integrated firm.

Our business spans the full lifecycle of technology — from research, design and development to supply, installation, commissioning and ongoing maintenance. We work with clients across manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, logistics, banking, energy and virtually every other sector that benefits from automation and intelligent systems.

We believe technology should work for people — reducing cost, improving quality, increasing safety and unlocking new capabilities. That is the core philosophy driving every project we take on.
